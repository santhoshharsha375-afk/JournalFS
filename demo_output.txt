 DEMO 3: directories, rename, truncate, delete
================================================================
$ journalfs demo.img mkdir /data
$ journalfs demo.img write /data/note.txt 'inside a subdir'
$ journalfs demo.img ls /data
note.txt
$ journalfs demo.img mv /report.txt /data/final.txt   (rename)
$ journalfs demo.img ls /data
note.txt
final.txt
$ journalfs demo.img truncate /data/final.txt 5
$ journalfs demo.img read /data/final.txt
Hello
$ journalfs demo.img rm /data/final.txt
$ journalfs demo.img rmdir /data
$ journalfs demo.img ls /
.
..

 DEMO 9 (optional, native Linux only -- not /mnt/c): FUSE mount
================================================================

  ./build/mkfs_journalfs ~/jfs.img
  ./build/journalfs ~/jfs.img ~/jfsmnt -f &    # -f = foreground
  echo hi > ~/jfsmnt/a.txt; cat ~/jfsmnt/a.txt; ls ~/jfsmnt
  fusermount3 -u ~/jfsmnt
